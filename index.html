Remote Patient Monitoring

Bring the hospital home — without filling another ward

Align eCare turns clinically-approved home devices into a live, auditable signal layer for each patient — blood pressure, glucose, oxygen saturation, and more — without adding one extra login or spreadsheet.

The full home monitoring device map

Blood pressure

Omron, A&D Medical, Withings, and iHealth cuffs ship with Bluetooth or Wi-Fi and clinically oriented accuracy workflows that can feed integration partners for API access.

Continuous glucose (CGM)

Dexcom G7 and Abbott FreeStyle Libre 3 stream glucose frequently, making it possible to normalize readings into one physician-facing signal layer for review.

Oxygen & wearables

Nonin and Masimo oximeters, Withings wearables, and mobile ECG devices can extend monitoring to SpO₂, ECG, HRV, and sleep for hospital-at-home style workflows.

One integration to get them all

A unified device layer can help Align ingest BP, CGM, SpO₂, weight, and ECG data in one schema — reducing one-off vendor friction and fragmented monitoring logic.
